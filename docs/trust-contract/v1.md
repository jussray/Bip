# Se'kret Bip Trust Contract v1

## Promise

> What I write is private unless I deliberately share a specific thing; safety help is a separate, clearly explained path.

## Invariants

1. Private child-authored content is private by default.
2. A caregiver relationship is not permission to read private child content.
3. Sharing must name the exact resource, exact recipient, allowed scope, and current grant state.
4. Revocation must end future access through every governed read path.
5. Safety help is a separate explicit action. A private journal write does not itself authorize disclosure, caregiver notification, external moderation, or a broader safety payload.
6. Privileged/service-role code must recreate the same authorization decision before reading or returning sensitive content.
7. Private-content-derived metadata must not become a caregiver inference channel.
8. Private child text must not enter telemetry, analytics, logs, notifications, exports, support tools, or AI/model requests unless a separately reviewed Trust Contract rule explicitly permits the exact operation.
9. The product must not claim live monitoring, guaranteed human response, diagnosis, or emergency dispatch unless those operations truly exist and have separate legal, clinical, and operational authority.
10. Trust-sensitive changes require negative proof, not only a happy-path or visual proof.

## v1 scope

In scope:

- private-by-default child-authored content;
- exact-resource, exact-recipient sharing when explicitly implemented;
- immediate revocation;
- age-appropriate ownership/share/safety disclosures;
- negative authorization, privileged-path, Realtime, egress, and telemetry tests.

Out of scope:

- diagnosis;
- predictive or continuous risk scoring;
- passive monitoring;
- automatic caregiver alerts derived from private writing;
- caregiver dashboards or private-activity feeds;
- clinician workflows;
- aggregate mood/trend sharing;
- social expansion.

## Safety boundary

Private reflection and safety support are separate states.

A child may explicitly open a support/safety path. Before any user-directed send, the product must identify the recipient, show the exact payload, explain what Bip can do, and state its operational limits.

Private journal content is not an implicit safety payload.

## Merge rule

A Trust Contract change may merge only when the exact candidate is supported by applicable database/API/runtime negative tests, its data-egress impact is reviewed, and current `main` authority is reacquired immediately before integration.
